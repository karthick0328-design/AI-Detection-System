What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering attack where malicious actors impersonate trustworthy entities such as banks, cloud providers, or coworkers.

Common signals include artificial urgency ("within 24 hours"), generic greetings, spelling discrepancies, and suspicious URLs with numeric IP addresses or mismatched subdomains.

HTTPS vs HTTP: Why Encryption Matters

HTTPS provides encrypted communication between your browser and the server, protecting against eavesdropping.

However, modern attackers can easily obtain free SSL certificates. Therefore, a padlock icon only guarantees encryption — it does not guarantee that the entity running the website is benign!

Look-alike Domains & Typosquatting

Attackers register domains that look identical to genuine brands (e.g. replacing 'l' with '1', or adding subdomains like paypal.com.verify-login.net).

Always inspect the root domain before entering sensitive credentials.
